Perception · Cognition · Computation

How do we make sense
of a multisensory world?

I study how the mind combines uncertain information to perceive, attend, and remember.

I’m Haocheng, a Ph.D. student in Computational Cognition at UCLA. I work with the Multisensory Processing Lab, advised by Ladan Shams.

My research combines psychophysical experiments and Bayesian modeling to understand how sensory signals are integrated—and how the brain infers whether they belong together. I also develop open-source tools that make these models accessible to other researchers.

01

Research interests

I.

Multisensory perception

How do we combine what we see and hear? I study causal inference across time, number, and other sensory dimensions.

II.

Dynamic Bayesian inference

Recursive and nonparametric models of how perception and attention adapt to changing sensory environments.

III.

Memory & cognition

Understanding reconstructive memory and suggestibility as inference under uncertainty.
