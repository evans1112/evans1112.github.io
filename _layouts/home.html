Haocheng Zhu 朱昊诚

Ph.D. student in Computational Cognition · UCLA

I study how the brain combines sensory information and makes inferences under uncertainty. My research brings together psychophysical experiments and Bayesian modeling to understand multisensory perception, attention, and memory.

At UCLA, I work in the Multisensory Processing Lab with Ladan Shams.

CV (PDF) Email Google Scholar

Research interests

Multisensory perception

Causal inference across sensory modalities, time, number, and other stimulus dimensions.

Dynamic Bayesian inference

Recursive and nonparametric models of how perception and attention adapt to changing sensory environments.

Memory & cognition

Models of reconstructive memory and suggestibility as inference under uncertainty.
